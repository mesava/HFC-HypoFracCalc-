## 2. Пять оригинальных работ с проверенным контекстом, но НЕ закрытыми параметрами

- [Denham 1995 PMID7569021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7569021/): primary Abstract describes **61 palliative head-neck patients**, telecaesium source, 10 fractions over 2 weeks at various dose rates, **alpha/beta range 7–10 Gy** and monoexponential T½ 0.27–0.5h. It **does not print** BCR 2025 estimate **9.3 [5.8,17.9]**; source formula/table needed. In HFC this is automatic default `defaultEligible:true`, so primary numerical provenance is a high-priority scientific gate, not assumed solved.
- [Turesson & Thames 1989 PMID2762590](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2762590/): original postmastectomy skin prospective study covers **750 fields / 450 patients**, once-/multiple-per-day and early/late endpoints, acute alpha/beta **range 7.5–11.2** but **not exact 8.8 [6.9,11.6]** printed in primary abstract. Important overall-time and incomplete repair confounding.
- [Bentzen Turesson Thames 1990 PMID2367694](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2367694/): graded late telangiectasia model has **401 fields / 335 patients**, 4 response grades and multi-year latency (90% mature damage 6.6/9.1/14.8 y across different grades); author Abstract does not publish specific **2.6 [2.2,3.3]** α/β printed in BCR Table10.1. Do not substitute separate Bentzen/Overgaard 1991 *telangiectasia* value 2.8 [−0.1,8.1].
- [Bentzen & Overgaard 1991 PMID1852907](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1852907/): **229 postmastectomy patients**; original Abstract explicitly prints *telangiectasia* alpha/beta **2.8 [−0.1,8.1]**, but HFC record concerns **subcutaneous fibrosis** 1.7 [0.6,2.6]. Separate endpoint is mentioned but its α/β point/CI **NOT source-print confirmed** from abstract. No mistaken transfer allowed.
- [Bentzen Skoczylas Bernier 2000 PMID10815624](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10815624/): original literature review emphasizes early pneumonitis vs later lung fibrosis, pneumonitis time rate approximately 0.5 Gy/day, lung fibrosis low α/β possibly 2–3 Gy. It **does not print** HFC early lung pneumonitis α/β=**4.0 [2.2,5.8] Gy**. Secondary BCR 2025 Table10.1 shows it, but independent source coefficient and 95% limit remain unverified.

## 3. Scientific blockers and the meaning of this audit

All 13 **original-source PDF raw binaries are missing from user-supplied provenance**, and web author abstracts cannot substitute for full published formula/table, endpoint/time dependence, independent fitting, source CI definition, or clinician/physicist review. Some published α/β values themselves are *effective* regression coefficients, not transferable α/β constants. Separate unresolved critical P3-C03 Hinata tBED/EQD₂ and P3-C07 deprecated direct-selection behavior.

**8 records have stronger limited external primary source verification; 5 remain numeric/CI pending.** This does *not* mean 98 clinically validated evidence records out of 103. No source coefficient, uncertainty, default, status or radiobiological algorithm changed. Scientific dataset `2026.10-v0.2` remains `draft`, no clinical release.
